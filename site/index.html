Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

What information we collect

When you place an order, we collect your name, phone number, city, delivery address, and any notes you provide through the order form on order.html.

How we use your information

Your order details are sent securely to the business owner via our order-processing endpoint and delivered to the owner's Telegram. The owner uses this information solely to contact you and process your order.

Data storage

This website does not maintain a permanent order database. Your cart is stored only in your browser's localStorage (key: tsi_cart) and is cleared after a successful order submission. We do not store your personal details after the order is sent.

Cookies and local storage

  • Cart contents while you browse — tsi_cart
  • Cookie consent preference — tsi_consent
  • Announcement bar dismissal — session only

No analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies are used.

Third-party services

Order notifications are delivered via the Telegram Bot API. Product images are loaded from Unsplash. Fonts from Google Fonts. Icons from Font Awesome (cdnjs). These services have their own privacy policies.
