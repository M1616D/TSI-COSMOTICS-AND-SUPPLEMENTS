Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

What we collect

When you place an order, we collect your name, phone number, city, delivery address, and any notes you provide in the order form.

How we use it

Your order details are sent to our secure order endpoint (/api/order) and delivered to the business owner's Telegram. We use this information only to contact you and process your order.

Data storage

This website does not maintain a permanent order database. Your cart is stored only in your browser's localStorage (key: tsi_cart) and is cleared after a successful order. We do not retain your personal details after the order message is delivered.

Cookies & local storage

No analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies are used.
